WHO WE ARE

Bridging science and application

KASA Lab (Knowledge-based Advanced Solutions Association) is a research and engineering group led by Prof. Alibakhsh Kasaeian, focused on developing knowledge-driven solutions to complex challenges in energy, water, and advanced engineering systems.

Our work brings together scientific research, computational modelling, numerical simulation, fluid mechanics, programming, artificial intelligence, optimization, and experimental approaches to investigate technical challenges and develop practical solutions.

Beyond academic research, KASA Lab engages with researchers, universities, and industry partners on research projects, computational studies, engineering analysis, simulation, data-driven modelling, and technology development.
