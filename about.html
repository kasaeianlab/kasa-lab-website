WHO WE ARE

Bridging science and application

KASA Lab (Knowledge-based Advanced Solutions Association) is a research and engineering group led by Prof. Alibakhsh Kasaeian, focused on developing knowledge-driven solutions to complex challenges in energy, water, and advanced engineering systems.

Our work brings together scientific research, computational modeling, numerical simulation, fluid mechanics, programming, artificial intelligence, optimization, and experimental approaches to develop practical and innovative solutions.

Beyond academic research, KASA Lab collaborates with researchers, universities, and industry partners on research projects, computational studies, fluid-flow analysis, engineering analysis, simulation, data-driven modeling, and technology development.
