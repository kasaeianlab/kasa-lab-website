bio: Alibakhsh Kasaeian is a Full Professor at the University of Tehran and Head
  of the KASA Lab, with over **300 scientific publications**, more than **13,000
  citations**, and an **h-index of 67**. He has been recognized among the
  world's **top 1% and 2%** of scientists for **seven consecutive years**, holds
  18 patents, and has authored 10 books. With 25 years of academic and
  industrial experience, he has held several senior managerial positions and
  served on the editorial boards of **three WoS-indexed journals**. His
  international achievements include **six years** of research collaboration
  with **NTUT in Taiwan**, delivering **six training workshops** there,
  conducting a workshop in **Kuwait** in collaboration with **MIT**, and
  completing a research project for the Swiss company **SMTEAM**. He has also
  been recognized as the **Faculty's Outstanding Researcher** and at the
  University of Tehran's International Festival.
