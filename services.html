PROJECT OUTPUTS

Deliverables are defined for each project according to its scope, technical requirements, available inputs, and intended use. Depending on the project, outputs may include:

Documented methods, assumptions, results, limitations, and technical interpretation.

Simulation Results

Numerical results, visualisations, parameter comparisons, and relevant model outputs.

Data Analysis

Processed data, statistical summaries, visualisations, and interpretation of results.

Computational Models

Model files, implementation details, or reproducible workflows when included in the agreed scope.

Feasibility Assessments

Technical comparisons, key constraints, and findings to support informed decisions.

Research Documentation

Methods, analysis procedures, and technical findings prepared for the intended project purpose.
